# SnowFree

A loose snowboard is on the run at Black Pine Resort. Flatten skiers, dodge rocks and rough snow, launch off rainbow ramps, and outrun the ski patrol.

**Play:** https://raw.githack.com/disnu/snowfree-game/main/index.html

## Controls

- **Left/right arrows or A D:** steer
- **Down arrow or S:** brake
- **Space / left-click / tap:** small hop over rocks, stumps, and rough snow
- **P:** pause

Acceleration is automatic. Going straight downhill builds speed, and steering scrubs it. Faster hits and escapes score more.

## Rules

- Skier: **100 points**, multiplied by speed at impact
- Dump skiers to raise your wanted level. Ski patrol arrives 4 seconds after your first hit.
- Lose the patrol for **8 seconds** to clear your stars and earn **500 points per star**.
- Rocks and trees damage the board. Dry trees catch fire. Rough snow slows you.
- Rainbow ramps are rare and give a big speed boost plus a clear landing lane.
- Board condition at zero ends the run. Getting caught below 10 km/h ends it too.

index.html is a single self-contained file. Download it and double-click to play offline, or open the link above.
